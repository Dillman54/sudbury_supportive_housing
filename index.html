Quick Eligibility Check

Are You Ready for
Supportive Housing?

If you meet these four core requirements, you're ready to begin your application. Our team will guide you through every step of the process.

✓

30+ Days Sober

You must have at least 30 days of documented sobriety from alcohol and substances. This can be verified through treatment programs, detox facilities, or recovery support services.

✓

18 Years or Older

Legal adult age requirement for residency. Our program is designed for adults ready to take responsibility for their recovery journey.

✓

Sudbury Connection

You must be a resident of Greater Sudbury or have established ties to the community such as family, support services, employment, or treatment programs.

✓

Recovery Commitment

Active engagement in recovery through counselling, 12-step groups, outpatient programs, or mental health supports. Ready to participate in house meetings and programming.

Simple Application

Your Path to
Stable Housing

From application to move-in, we make the process straightforward and supportive. Most applicants complete the entire process within 1-2 weeks.

Step 1

Submit Application

Complete our intake form online or with help from your case worker. Include proof of 30-day sobriety and any supporting documents.

Step 2

Phone Screening

Brief 15-minute call to confirm eligibility, answer your questions, and schedule your intake interview.

Step 3

Intake Interview

Meet with our team to discuss your recovery goals, housing needs, and develop your initial support plan.

Step 4

Acceptance & Move-In

Receive your acceptance letter, sign the Sober Living Agreement, and move into your new home with orientation support.

Program Expectations

What We Ask
Of Our Residents

Success in supportive housing requires commitment. Here's what we expect from every resident to maintain a safe, supportive environment for all.

Resident Commitments

📋

Program Participation

Attend weekly house meetings, engage with your case manager, participate in life skills programming

🚫

Maintain Sobriety

Zero tolerance for substances on-site, agree to random screening, commitment to abstinence

🤝

Respect Community

Follow house rules, respect quiet hours, contribute to chores, support fellow residents

💰

Financial Responsibility

Pay program fees on time ($900/month), work with staff on budgeting, save for future housing

🎯

Recovery Plan

Work on personal recovery goals, attend counselling or groups, prepare for independent living

✅

Follow Guidelines

Respect curfews, visitor policies, maintain room cleanliness, comply with safety protocols
